## Checks against the rules

- **An event in every shot:** yes (A the jump; B Up wins and $1; C the books fill; D the mint; E the bites; F the tip; G the reveal and buy; H the burn; I the crumble and reset).
- **Beats every 5–8 s:** 2.9 · 7.1 · 9.9 · 11.3 · 13.2 · 15.2 · 17.8 · 22.4 · 25.8 · 32.1 · 36.2 · 40.6 · 43.5 · 50.6 · 55.8 · 58.0 · 61.5 · 63.6. The longest gap is 7.1 s (43.5→50.6), and it's filled by the "moves first" read.
- **Reads:** one at a time. Every new concept holds ≥ 1.2 s after it lands (62%, $1, the mint, 3%, the formula, BINANCE, barely moves it, the crumble).
- **No slides:** the books have no row labels, and every on-screen number appears only while it's being said (80¢, 62¢/62%, 60¢, 40¢, 0%, 3%, 50/50, the formula, Price to Beat, 0.5 s).
- **Hook promise kept:** "watching another screen" → BINANCE at 43.5, how it's used, the twist that burns people who chase it, and the loop.
- **Safe zone:** key action in y 200–1110; captions at y 1190; tags at 1330; nothing below 1440 or right of 918 that matters.
- **Truth:** every number maps to `sources.md`. The hook's 50→80¢ is illustrative and the card says "example". Fees are "up to 3%" (the published rate, since personal discounts exist). "Limitless says" is attributed. Binance is "one of the fastest places". The average is 60 s, and a tie goes to Up ("at or above").

## What changed from TOPIC.md

1. **Fees:** "cheap long shots cost the most" became "up to 3%" for the taker. The published buy fee is a flat 3% from 1¢ to 50¢ (docs), so long shots don't cost more than a 50¢ buy. The fee beat is now part of the same trade (the stranger was the taker).
2. **TWAP window:** named as 60 seconds. It's also the Price to Beat's source ("where it started" in B, named in G).
3. **Risk beat:** adds the 0.5 s taker delay (from the live market's `takerDelayMs`).
4. **Binance:** "one of the fastest places Bitcoin moves show up". The research is mixed on spot versus perps leading.
5. **Length:** 65.6 s rather than ~60. That's the price of explaining eight mechanisms at the research-measured 3.8 words/s with air after each wow beat.
