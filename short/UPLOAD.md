## Title

This Bitcoin bet jumped 30¢ in seconds. Here's who saw it first

(Alternative: "15-minute Bitcoin bets aren't coin flips")

## Description

A 15-minute Bitcoin "Up or Down" market, from open to close: why a price is a probability, how two buy orders with no seller get a brand-new pair minted, who pays fees, why traders watch a faster exchange, and why a last-second spike barely counts toward a 60-second average.

Not financial advice. This is an education video. Prediction markets can lose you your whole stake: fees, latency (taker orders wait half a second), and a settlement price (Chainlink's 60-second average) that isn't the exchange price you're watching.

Unofficial explainer. Not affiliated with, made by or endorsed by Limitless, Binance or Chainlink. The prices on screen are illustrative examples. The fee figures are Limitless's published rates as checked on 25 Sep 2026, and your own rate may differ.
