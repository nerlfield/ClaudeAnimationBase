> Before anyone asks: this is what you'd see if your rockets held you still just above the edge. If you fell in freely, the sky would *not* shrink to a dot (Andrew Hamilton at JILA has simulations of both). Also, the dot's rim and the black hole's dark edge at the start are made of the same light: rays skimming the photon sphere at 1.5× the horizon.
